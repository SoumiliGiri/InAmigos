Who We Are

A Movement Rooted in Compassion

Founded by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit licensed by the Central Government of India. Operating from Chhattisgarh, we work across the country to address critical societal challenges through a network of professionals and volunteers.

We hold 80G & 12A certifications ensuring full transparency and tax-exempt benefits for donors, and are CSR-1 registered for corporate partnerships. Our work is aligned with national goals through NITI Aayog registration and certified to ISO 9001:2015 standards.
